How Input Automation stages content for Output Automation:

1. Every RSS source has a designated gdriveFolderId.
2. When an article is fetched, the AI/TTS renderer produces a 9:16 MP4 video reel with audio narration and viral hashtags.
3. The video is uploaded to the Google Drive folder via resumable Google Drive API v3.
4. The article status is updated to uploaded_to_drive in MongoDB Atlas.
5. Output Automation scans this Drive folder on its own cron schedule and broadcasts each video to Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, and Telegram!
